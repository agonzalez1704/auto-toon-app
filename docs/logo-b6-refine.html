5. My recommendation

Ship B6.1 (Pure Frames).

  • Survives all the way down to 28px (favicon). B6.2's cutout collapses below 48px.
  • Reads as butterfly silhouette AND two polaroids — works whether viewer projects "wings" or "photos".
  • Simplest possible execution. Easier to embroider, animate, license-protect.
  • Amber + violet split = on-brand without needing a third color.
